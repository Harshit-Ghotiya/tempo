TEMPO for macOS

1. Double-click Tempo.app.

2. macOS will probably say it cannot check Tempo for malicious software. That is because Tempo is not
   from the App Store and is not notarised by Apple (notarising needs a paid developer account).
   Tempo only listens on your own computer and never sends your video anywhere.
   Allow it once:
   - macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later: open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message
     about Tempo, click "Open Anyway", then confirm.
   - macOS 14 or earlier: right-click (or Control-click) Tempo.app, choose Open, then click Open again.

3. If macOS says Tempo is "damaged and can't be opened": open Terminal, type   xattr -cr   followed by a
   space, drag Tempo.app into the Terminal window, press Return, then open Tempo.app again.
   (This only removes the "downloaded from the internet" flag.)

4. Your browser opens at http://localhost:8000 . If it does not, type that address yourself.
   Allow the camera when the browser asks. Safari, Chrome and Edge work.

5. The very first run downloads two small model files (about 10 MB) from Google's MediaPipe host.
   After that Tempo works without internet.

6. To quit, close the browser tab. Tempo stops by itself about 3 minutes later.
   If macOS asks whether Tempo may accept incoming connections, click Allow: it only listens on this computer.

If something goes wrong, the log is at ~/Library/Caches/Tempo/tempo.log
