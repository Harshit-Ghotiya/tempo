TEMPO for Linux (64-bit)

1. In a terminal:  chmod +x Tempo && ./Tempo
2. Your browser opens at http://localhost:8000 . Allow the camera.
3. The very first run downloads two small model files (about 10 MB) from Google's MediaPipe host.
   After that Tempo works without internet.
4. To quit, close the browser tab. Tempo stops by itself about 3 minutes later (or press Ctrl+C).
Log: ~/.cache/Tempo/tempo.log
