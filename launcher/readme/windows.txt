TEMPO for Windows

1. Double-click Tempo.exe.
   Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because Tempo is not signed with a paid certificate.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

2. A small window opens and your browser opens at http://localhost:8000 . If it does not, type that address.
   Allow the camera when the browser asks.

3. The very first run downloads two small model files (about 10 MB) from Google's MediaPipe host.
   After that Tempo works without internet.

4. To quit, close the browser tab (the Tempo window closes by itself about 3 minutes later) or close the window.

If something goes wrong, the log is at %LocalAppData%\Tempo\tempo.log
